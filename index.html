ABOUT / MEET THE FOUNDER

Built on Three Decades of Expertise.

The name Merwede is drawn from a river in the Netherlands, a nod to our founder’s roots and a reflection of our core philosophy: that the most complex challenges are often solved through steady, focused, and quiet expertise.

Merwede Architecture is led by Paul van der Merwe, a SACAP-registered architect with over 30 years of hands-on experience in architectural design and project delivery. Paul has personally overseen more than 120 building projects, from bespoke private residences to commercial developments, across the Western Cape and Gauteng.

His approach is methodical without being rigid, creative without losing sight of practicality. Every project at Merwede carries his direct involvement: from the first sketch to the final municipal submission.

What sets us apart

  • Client collaboration at every stage of the design process
  • Photorealistic 3D renderings that bring your vision to life before a single brick is laid
  • Full management of municipal submissions and regulatory compliance
  • A consistent commitment to budget adherence and timeline integrity
  • Sustainable and innovative design practices embedded from the outset

What We Do.

We offer a full suite of architectural services tailored to residential and commercial clients, whether you are building from the ground up or transforming an existing space.

Start Your Project

Architectural Design

From concept sketches to detailed construction drawings, we develop designs that are functional, beautiful, and buildable. Every design decision is guided by your brief, your site, and your budget.

3D Visualisation & Rendering

Our photorealistic 3D renders allow you to experience your future space before construction begins. This is not just a presentation tool. It is a decision-making tool that saves time, money, and costly misalignment down the line.

Municipal Submissions & Approvals

Navigating local authority requirements can be one of the most frustrating parts of any build. We manage the full submission process, drawing packages, compliance documentation, and liaison with municipal bodies so you do not have to.

Project Coordination

We work closely with contractors, engineers, and other consultants to ensure that what is designed is what gets built. Our involvement does not end at the drawing board.

Luxury Residential Architecture

Our core specialisation is luxury residential architecture, homes designed around how you live, built to a standard that endures. Whether a new build on an undeveloped stand or a significant extension to an existing property, we bring the same level of care to every project.

OUR PROCESS

A Clear Process.
No Surprises.

Architecture should feel exciting, not overwhelming. Here is how we work. From first conversation to the final handover.

01

Initial Consultation

We start with a conversation. We listen to your vision, assess your site, and align on scope of work, budget, and timeline. No jargon, no pressure.

02

Concept Design

We develop initial design concepts and present them through sketches and 3D renderings. You see your project come to life and we refine until it is exactly right.

03

Design Development & Documentation

Once the design is approved, we develop full technical drawings and documentation. This is the foundation contractors build from. Precision here prevents costly problems on site.

04

Municipal Submission

We compile and submit all required documentation to the relevant local authority, managing the approval process on your behalf.

05

Construction Support

We remain available throughout construction, reviewing progress, addressing site queries, and ensuring the build stays true to the approved design.
